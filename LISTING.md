## Title
Swole Clicker

## Short description (one line)
Tap any muscle to train it and grow your lifter from noodle arms to Mr. Swoleympia.

## Description
Swole Clicker is an idle gym game where every muscle counts. Tap any of 15 muscles on your lifter — chest,
biceps, lats, glutes and more — and watch him do a real exercise for it: curls, bench press, pull-ups,
deadlifts, hip thrusts and 30 more moves. Each muscle grows on its own, so train evenly for a symmetry bonus
or go full Chicken Legs. It's your call.

Buy gym gear that keeps training while you're away, fill the pump bar to go Beast Mode, and catch gym events
like protein deliveries, rep storms and your gym crush watching you lift. As you rank up you move from Dad's
Garage to Mom's Basement, a real gym, a hardcore dungeon and finally the Swoleympia stage, each with its own
soundtrack.

Challenge 8 gym rivals in arm-wrestling, perfect-form, hold-the-weight and pose-off minigames to win titles
and cosmetics. When you're huge, hit the stage: pose for three judges, earn trophies and spend them on
permanent perks for your next season.

Features:
- 15 tappable muscles with front and back views and 35 animated exercises
- 37 pieces of gear, 59 upgrades and item milestones
- Beast Mode, gym events and golden shakes
- 8 rivals with 4 different minigames
- Prestige with a judged posing show and 14 trophy perks
- 90 awards, daily quests and login streaks
- 5 gyms with their own music, plus a locker full of silly cosmetics

## Controls
- Tap / click a muscle on the lifter to train it
- Space: do a rep on the selected muscle
- T: turn around (front / back view)
- Click the shop, upgrades and other tabs to buy and manage everything
