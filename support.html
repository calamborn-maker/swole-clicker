Common questions

Where is my progress saved? On your device. In the iPhone app it's kept until you delete the app. In a browser you can back it up from ⚙️ Settings → Export, and restore it with Import.

How do I play? Tap any muscle on the lifter to train it. Tap fast to fill the pump bar and go Beast Mode, buy gear in the Shop, and use 🔄 to turn around and train your back.

How do I turn on music or turn off vibration? Tap ⚙️ in the top corner.

Something went wrong / I lost my save. Email us with your device model and what happened.
