Privacy Policy — Swole Clicker

Last updated: October 6, 2026.

This Privacy Policy explains how Swole Clicker (the "Game"), operated by Dumped ("we", "us"), handles information when you play the Game. Contact: dumpeddaily703@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect

Game progress. Your save — gains, items, achievements, cosmetics and settings — is stored on your device (browser localStorage) and, when you play on a game portal such as CrazyGames, via that portal's cloud-save feature (the CrazyGames SDK Data Module) associated with your portal account. You can export, import, or reset your save at any time from the in-game ⚙️ Settings panel.

Portal profile. When you play on CrazyGames while logged in, the Game shows your CrazyGames username and profile picture in the corner of the screen. This is read from the CrazyGames SDK at runtime only; we do not store it.

We do not collect personal information (no accounts, names or emails) ourselves.

iOS app. The Swole Clicker app for iPhone shows no ads, has no accounts or analytics, and collects no data. Your save is stored only on your device and is removed if you delete the app.

2. Advertising

When played on a game portal, the Game may display ads (including rewarded video and interstitial ads) served by the host portal and its advertising partners — for example CrazyGames and/or GameDistribution. These partners may use cookies, device identifiers, and similar technologies to serve and measure ads, including personalized (interest-based) advertising where permitted.

Information processed by ad partners is governed by their own privacy policies:

  • CrazyGames: crazygames.com/privacy
  • GameDistribution: gamedistribution.com/privacy-policy
  • Google: policies.google.com/technologies/ads

3. Your choices (consent & GDPR/CCPA)

Where required by law (e.g. EEA/UK GDPR, California CCPA), our ad partners present a consent dialog before personalized ads are shown, and you may withdraw or change consent. You can also opt out of personalized advertising via youronlinechoices.eu (EU), optout.aboutads.info (US), or your device/browser ad settings.

4. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. Ad partners are instructed to serve only non-personalized ads to users where required.

5. Data retention & security

Your save stays on your device (and, on a portal, in that portal's cloud-save) until you clear it. We do not operate our own servers or hold a separate copy. No method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

6. Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be posted on this page with a new date.
